# Prepare for Section 3

Use this page to check account and app access. You will download, upload and check your personal approval tracker during [Exercise 3 Practice 1](../exercises/03-document-approval.md#practice-1-prepare-your-approval-tracker); workbook setup is not required before class.

1. Sign in with the training account confirmed by the organiser and select the agreed Power Automate environment.
2. Confirm with the instructor that the account can use **Approvals** and create a cloud flow. Environment provisioning must be handled by an authorised administrator; do not create an environment or start a trial yourself.
3. Confirm access to **OneDrive for Business** and **Excel for the web**, including uploading and reading/writing a personal workbook.
4. Confirm that the same account can send and receive **Office 365 Outlook** email. You will use your own mailbox for requester and approver.

> **Access:** Approvals, Excel Online (Business) and Office 365 Outlook are Standard connectors. The organiser must confirm the required licences, provisioning, permissions and actual learner access.

The exercise uses one manual **Request title** input; no new Form or business-document upload is required. Continue to [Practice 1: Prepare your approval tracker](../exercises/03-document-approval.md#practice-1-prepare-your-approval-tracker). If access is unavailable, follow the instructor's demonstration and identify which hands-on check remains unperformed.
