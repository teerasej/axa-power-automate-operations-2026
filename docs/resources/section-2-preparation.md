# Prepare your Form and workbook before Section 2

Complete this with the organiser before 10:45. Preparation is outside the 75-minute exercise so you can build and test at a steady pace.

## Your personal workbook

1. Download [operations-request-tracker.xlsx](/downloads/operations-request-tracker.xlsx).
2. Open OneDrive for Business with your training account. Create this folder:

   ```text
   PowerAutomateTraining
   ```

3. Upload the workbook. Check worksheet `Requests`, table `RequestsTable` and columns RequestId, Title, Description, RequesterEmail, Category and Status.
4. Leave the initial blank row empty; do not manually enter the test requests. Close the workbook before testing to avoid concurrent edits. Each learner uses a personal copy.

## Your personal Form

1. Open Microsoft Forms with the same account. Select **New Form** and enter:

   ```text
   Operations Practice Request
   ```

2. Add four questions with these exact names. Set every question to **Required**:

   - **Text**, short answer:

     ```text
     Task title
     ```

   - **Text**, with **Long answer** enabled:

     ```text
     Description
     ```

   - **Text**, for your own training email address:

     ```text
     Requester email
     ```

     We collect this explicitly rather than assuming Responder email is available for every account.

   - **Choice**, with **Multiple answers** and **Other** disabled:

     ```text
     Category
     ```

3. Enter these two Category options, one per option:

   ```text
   Operations
   Finance
   ```

4. Check that response settings permit your training account to submit. Open **Preview** and check all four questions and the two Category options. If the organiser supplies a Form copy, confirm you actually own or can access your copy. Do not connect every learner's flow to the instructor's Form.

## Readiness checkpoint

- You can open and preview your Form; Category has exactly two single-choice options.
- You can open your personal workbook and see RequestsTable.
- The organiser has confirmed your mailbox, cloud-flow entitlement and connector access.

These services require appropriate Microsoft 365 access. Standard connectors do not mean every service is free; confirm licences before training.

## Recovery

If a Form is missing from the picker, check connection account, ownership and Form Id with the instructor. If unresolved, use the demonstration rather than expanding permissions yourself.

If the workbook is missing, upload a personal copy and reselect File/Table. Recheck all six mappings in an existing flow. Use the [reset workbook](./downloads.md#reset) if necessary. Renaming or replacing a file does not guarantee the flow reference changes. Do not create a shared class workbook as a shortcut. If access remains unavailable, record demonstration participation rather than hands-on completion.
