# Check account and app access before Section 2

Check access with the organiser before Section 2. Workbook preparation and Form creation are included in the exercise; you do not need to complete either setup task beforehand.

## Account and app checks

- Open Power Automate with your training account and select the instructor-confirmed environment.
- Open [Microsoft Forms](https://forms.office.com) with the same account and confirm you can create a personal Form.
- Open [Microsoft 365](https://www.microsoft365.com), then **OneDrive** from the app launcher. Confirm you can upload a personal file and open Excel for the web.
- Confirm that Outlook opens your authorised training mailbox.
- Ask the organiser to confirm your cloud-flow entitlement, connector access and permissions. These services require appropriate Microsoft 365 access; Standard connectors do not mean every service is free.

## Setup during Exercise 2

1. [Practice 1: Prepare your Excel workbook](../exercises/02-forms-to-excel.md#practice-1-prepare-your-excel-workbook) - download and upload the tracker, then verify its existing Table and columns.
2. [Practice 2: Create your request Form](../exercises/02-forms-to-excel.md#practice-1-create-your-request-form) - create and preview the four required questions and two routing options.

## If access is unavailable

Ask the instructor to check the training account and permissions. Use the prepared demonstration if the issue cannot be resolved. Record this as a walkthrough, not a completed learner run. Do not switch to an unauthorised account or create a shared class workbook as a shortcut.
