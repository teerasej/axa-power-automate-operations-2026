# Exercise 4: Flow Rescue Challenge

<PracticeIllustration src="/day-2/images/rescue-overview.webp" :width="1536" :height="864" alt="A team investigates a small problem and exchanges checked test evidence with another team." loading="eager" />

Your team will repair **one** small Flow. Other teams work on different problems at the same time. After two tests, exchange evidence with another team and explain your repair.

Every challenge includes optional progressive hints and a highlighted **Solution available** section. Select **View step-by-step solution** whenever you want the exact repair and both verification tests.

> **Draft rehearsal:** The revised starters are being checked one at a time. Use only the baseline or package assigned for your rehearsal. Setup and live repair cannot be accepted until the assigned starting route and learner checks pass.

## Roles and timing

Choose a driver, checker and reporter. In a pair, the checker also reports.

| Time | What to do |
|---|---|
| 14:45–15:00 | Hear the briefing and open your assigned, prepared Flow |
| 15:00–15:35 | Diagnose, make one repair, run two tests and exchange evidence |
| 15:35–15:50 | Five two-minute reports, with five minutes for transitions |
| 15:50–16:00 | Write one transfer note and close |

Complete your assigned card's **Setup** before the repair timer starts. Each card has one faulty setting in one action. You may need several clicks to change that setting; you do not need to rebuild the Flow.

| Assigned card | Your repair topic | Service used |
|---|---|---|
| [R1. Office Script result](../challenges/01-office-script.md) | A script's table-name parameter | Excel Online (Business) and a supplied Office Script |
| [R2. File dependency order](../challenges/02-file-sequencing.md) | An action's Run after setting | OneDrive for Business |
| [R3. Email image resource](../challenges/03-html-image.md) | An image URL | Outlook |
| [R4. Decision gate simulation](../challenges/04-approval-gate.md) | A Condition comparison | Built-in actions |
| [R5. PDF output](../challenges/05-pdf-generation.md) | A source filename | OneDrive for Business |

Open only the card assigned to your team. Your instructor checks the required service access before assigning it.

## Practice 1: Repair one assigned Flow {#practice-1-repair-one-assigned-flow}

<PracticeIllustration src="/day-2/images/rescue-p01.webp" :width="1536" :height="864" alt="Several teams investigate different controlled Flow exceptions in parallel." />

Use the card's two tests to show that your one-setting repair works.

1. Open your assigned challenge page and complete its **Setup**, including the starting-route tab announced by the instructor. Check your assigned personal flow name and editing access before making changes.
2. Follow the card's first test without changing the faulty setting. Record the symptom shown on that card.
3. Follow its diagnostic steps. Use the progressive hints only when you need them.
4. Change the one setting supported by your evidence. Follow [Save your changes](../preparation.md#save-your-changes) before testing the edit.
5. Run tests A and B on the card. Check the actual output, not only the run status.
6. Record four short lines:

   ```text
   Original symptom:
   Setting changed:
   Test A result:
   Test B result:
   ```

### Checkpoint

Your team has one explained repair and two results that meet the card's checkpoint. If you reviewed captured examples instead of executing the Flow, label the result **Evidence review**.

## Practice 2: Verify another team’s test {#practice-2-verify-another-teams-test}

<PracticeIllustration src="/day-2/images/rescue-p02.webp" :width="1536" :height="786" alt="Two teams exchange test cards and compare the result evidence." />

Check one other team's output against its test input.

1. Exchange your four-line evidence note with the neighbouring team.
2. Open that team's challenge page and read its checkpoint.
3. Ask the team to show one test in its own authorised Flow and files. Compare its input with the resulting file, email or action output.
4. Record one verification statement:

   ```text
   Case checked:
   Output checked:
   Checkpoint met, or still missing:
   ```

5. Give the note back. Prepare a two-minute report: show the original symptom, name the setting changed and show the two results. Do not create presentation slides.
6. At the close, write one place in your own project where you could use the same diagnostic method.

### Checkpoint

Another team has checked one specific result. Your report clearly distinguishes a checked output from something that still needs a test.

## Close

Wait for active runs to finish. Turn off your training Flows when the instructor announces cleanup. Keep your synthetic test files and short evidence notes for reference.
