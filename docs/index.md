## Before you begin

- Open these portals with the training account confirmed by the organiser:

  1. [Power Automate](https://make.powerautomate.com/)
  2. [Outlook](https://outlook.office.com/mail/)
  3. [Microsoft Forms](https://forms.office.com/)
  4. [OneDrive for Business via Microsoft 365](https://www.microsoft365.com/) — select **Apps**, then **OneDrive**.

- Select the instructor's environment and use your own training email as recipient.
- Check account and app access with [Section 2 preparation](./resources/section-2-preparation.md) and download the [practice files](./resources/downloads.md).
- Confirm [Section 3 account and app access](./resources/section-3-preparation.md). Prepare your separate [approval tracker in Exercise 3 Practice 1](./exercises/03-document-approval.md#practice-1-prepare-your-approval-tracker).
- Use synthetic data only. Ask the instructor for help if you cannot access an app or create a connection.

> **Licences:** These exercises use Built-in actions and Standard connectors: Office 365 Outlook, Microsoft Forms, Excel Online (Business) and Approvals. No Premium connector is required, but Power Automate and the related service entitlements, environment provisioning and permissions must be confirmed. Standard does not mean every service is free. Use an organisation-approved generative AI app, including the IT-suggested SecureGPT; confirm access and licence requirements. No particular AI plan or model is prescribed.
