# Optional exercises — Try after class

Choose any exercise and work at your own pace. These activities are voluntary, with no deadline or submission. Each exercise includes all its preparation in **Practice 1**; you do not need to complete another exercise first.

| Exercise | What you will practise | Check your result |
|---|---|---|
| [Optional A — Compare Two Lists and Find Missing Records](./compare-lists.md) | Read two Excel lists, match IDs and record a result. | C001 Matched; C002 Missing. |
| [Optional B — Due-Date Reminders for Pending Tasks](./due-date-reminders.md) | Schedule a check, filter dates/status and send reminders to yourself. | Two eligible tasks receive email; two others are excluded. |
| [Optional C — Email-to-Document Register](./email-document-register.md) | Retrieve a PDF attachment, save it and record its location. | Two readable PDFs and two matching register rows. |
